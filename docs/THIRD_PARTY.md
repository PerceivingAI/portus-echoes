## 1. Core Transcription & Audio Components

### whisper.cpp
- **Description:** High-performance C/C++ port of OpenAI's Whisper model with Vulkan and CPU vector optimizations.
- **Author:** Georgi Gerganov and contributors
- **Version Pinned:** `v1.9.1`
- **License:** [MIT License](https://github.com/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE)

### whisper-rs
- **Description:** Rust bindings for whisper.cpp.
- **Author:** tazz4843 and contributors
- **License:** [MIT License](https://github.com/tazz4843/whisper-rs/blob/master/LICENSE)

### Silero VAD
- **Description:** Pre-trained enterprise-grade Voice Activity Detector.
- **Author:** Silero Team
- **Model Version:** `v6.2.0` (`ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin`)
- **License:** [MIT License](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad/blob/master/LICENSE)

### CPAL (Cross-Platform Audio Library)
- **Description:** Low-level cross-platform audio I/O library in pure Rust interfacing with WASAPI and ALSA/PipeWire.
- **Author:** The RustAudio Developers
- **License:** [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal/blob/master/LICENSE-APACHE) / [MIT License](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal/blob/master/LICENSE-MIT)

### Rubato
- **Description:** Highly optimized audio sample rate converter in Rust.
- **Author:** HEnquist and contributors
- **License:** [MIT License](https://github.com/HEnquist/rubato/blob/master/LICENSE)

### mp3lame-encoder
- **Description:** Safe Rust wrapper around LAME MP3 encoding library.
- **Author:** The RustAudio Developers
- **License:** [MIT License](https://github.com/RustAudio/mp3lame-encoder/blob/master/LICENSE)

### hound
- **Description:** WAV audio encoding and decoding library in Rust.
- **Author:** Ruud van Asseldonk
- **License:** [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/ruuda/hound/blob/master/LICENSE)
